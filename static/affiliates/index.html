§ HOW THE RATE WORKS

It grows with what you refer, and it never resets

Recommend the app to merchants, and for every one of them who subscribes, we pay you a percentage of the revenue we collect from their account. That percentage is guaranteed for as long as they stay subscribed, so a merchant you referred two years ago still earns you a share today.

The rate itself isn't fixed: it starts at 25% and climbs by 2.5% for every $100 of referred MRR (monthly recurring revenue) you've brought in, up to a cap of 40% once you reach $600 of average referred MRR. It's refreshed monthly based on your average referred MRR, so the more merchants you bring in, and the longer they stick around, the higher your share climbs.
