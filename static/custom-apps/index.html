§ WHO THIS IS FOR

You're probably in the right place if:

Plenty of automation problems are solved by Shopify Flow or a quick fix, and we'll tell you when that's the case. These are the ones where it stops being enough:

i.
Flow can't hold the logic

Nested conditions, cross-object checks, scoring: the kind of rule a trigger-and-action builder was never meant to run.

ii.
Your data moves faster than your tools

Catalog, inventory, or order changes that need a reaction in real time, not on the next scheduled sync.

iii.
You're past demo-data volume

Tens of thousands of SKUs, orders, or sync events: the scale most integrations weren't built, or tested, to hold.

iv.
A script won't cut it

You need something engineered, tested, and maintained like production software, not a one-off left running unattended.

v.
Staff need a UI, not a dev ticket

The logic can live in code, but the people updating it every day shouldn't need a developer on call for routine changes.

vi.
You need to know what changed, and why

Automation that quietly does the wrong thing is worse than none at all. We build in monitoring and a history log from day one, so nobody's left guessing why something changed.
